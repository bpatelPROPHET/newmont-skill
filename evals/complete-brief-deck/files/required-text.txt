Ramp-up status
Ahafo North is ahead of plan on throughput and on plan for cost
PURPOSE
This review confirms where the ramp-up stands against the approved plan and what we need from the executive team this quarter.
WHAT IS WORKING
Mill throughput is at 104% of plan
Commissioning finished two weeks early and the circuit has held nameplate since October.
Recordable injuries are down
TRIFR fell to 0.9 from 1.4 in the prior quarter with no high-potential incidents.
WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION
Power supply remains the key risk
Grid stability events caused 11 hours of unplanned downtime in October.
Contractor demobilization is behind
About 180 contractors remain on site against a plan of 120 by this point.
Three actions will keep the ramp-up on plan through the first quarter
Secure backup power
Commission the second genset bank by January and sign the grid firming agreement.
Complete contractor demobilization
Close out the remaining 60 contractor roles by mid-December with handover to operations.
Lock in the cost base
Hold AISC within 3% of plan by finishing the reagent contract renegotiation.
