TAILINGS MANAGEMENT
Every operating facility now meets the Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management
All 32 tailings storage facilities completed conformance review this quarter, two years ahead of the original commitment.
Independent review complete
Each facility was assessed by an independent tailings review board and the findings are published on the site pages.
Monitoring is now continuous
Piezometer and inclinometer data streams to the central dashboard every fifteen minutes with automated alerts.
Closure planning is funded
Closure cost estimates were updated for every facility and the provision is reflected in the Q3 accounts.
What this means for you
If you work on or near a tailings facility, the updated emergency preparedness plan applies from 1 December. Your site lead will walk you through it.
